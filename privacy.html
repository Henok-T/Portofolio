Privacy

Plain facts about what this site collects, why, and what you can do about it. Nothing is shared for advertising. There are no tracking scripts or cookies.

Last updated: 24 September 2026

What we collect

Inquiry form

When you submit the contact form we receive: your name, your email address, the type of work you need (chosen from a dropdown), your message, and — if you clicked a pricing plan button before submitting — which plan you were asking about.

Server request logs

Netlify, the company that hosts this site, automatically records a standard log entry for every page request. Each entry contains your IP address, the date and time, the URL you visited, your HTTP status code, and your browser and operating system. Asmara Web Design does not add to or separately store these logs.

Why

Form submissions are collected solely to reply to your inquiry. We do not use them for marketing, and we do not sell or share them with anyone outside of Netlify's infrastructure. Server logs exist because web hosting automatically produces them; they are used for diagnosing technical problems.

Where it goes

Form submissions are stored in Netlify's form dashboard and emailed to the studio owner. They stay within Netlify's systems. Server logs are held by Netlify. No data is sent to any other service.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for as long as they are needed to handle your inquiry. Once the conversation is finished and there is no ongoing business reason to keep it, we delete the submission from Netlify's dashboard. Server logs are retained according to Netlify's standard policy. You can ask us to delete your submission at any time — see How to contact us below.

What is stored in your browser

Draft saved in localStorage

As you type in the contact form, the site saves a draft to your browser's localStorage under the key asmara-inquiry-draft. It stores the four fields you are filling in: name, email, service type, and message. Nothing else is written there. The draft is cleared automatically when you submit successfully. It is never sent anywhere except through the form itself.

Service worker cache

The site installs a service worker that caches its own files in your browser — the HTML, stylesheet, JavaScript, fonts, and a few images — so the site loads quickly on repeat visits and works when you are offline. The cache contains only site files. It stores no personal data.

Third parties (Netlify)

Netlify is the only third-party service this site relies on. It provides hosting, form processing, and CDN delivery. Netlify processes form submissions and server logs under its own privacy policy. No other third-party services, analytics providers, or advertising networks are used on this site.

Links to LinkedIn and GitHub appear on the homepage. Clicking one takes you to that site; no data is sent to LinkedIn or GitHub unless you choose to visit them.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, and you can ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a few business days.

To remove the saved draft from your own browser, clear your browser's site data for this domain, or open your browser's developer tools, go to Application → Local Storage, and delete the asmara-inquiry-draft key.

How to contact us

The fastest way to reach us is LinkedIn. Send a direct message and we will reply within one business day.
