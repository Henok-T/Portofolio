About

Henok T.

Web developer and software support engineer in the Atlanta metro area.

I build websites and small applications, and I spend a large part of my week on production support: incidents, defects, and the unglamorous work of making software reliable for the people who depend on it.

My path into software was not linear. I trained first in law, then in radiography in Norway, then completed a software development certification at The Tech Academy in Portland. The common thread is process — understand the situation, document it, and fix the actual cause.

I speak English, Norwegian, and Tigrinya.
